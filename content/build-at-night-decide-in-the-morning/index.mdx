After years of remote work, my rule is simple: build at night, decide in the morning, and never merge anything I wrote after midnight without rereading it in daylight.

My best code gets written between 10pm and 1am. My best decisions get made at 9am. It took me embarrassingly long to stop confusing the two. Late-night me writes brilliant solutions to problems that morning me realizes we don't have.

At night nobody interrupts me, so I can hold a whole problem in my head and move fast. In the morning I'm slower, but much better at asking whether the thing should exist at all. Splitting the work that way keeps the speed and loses most of the regret.
