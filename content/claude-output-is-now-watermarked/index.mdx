If you ship a feature built on Claude, its output now carries a mark you did not add. That changes what you should tell your users. It does not give you a detection tool.

## What changed

Since 2 August, Anthropic has been watermarking Claude output globally, outside the EU too. Text gets an invisible statistical pattern that survives copy and paste. Generated .png, .jpg and .svg files get signed provenance metadata, which is closer to EXIF than to a visible badge. Models launched on or after that date support it at launch, and older ones are being retrofitted.

## Why it happened

The driver is Article 50 of the EU AI Act, which came into force the same day. Penalties go up to 15 million euro or 3 percent of global revenue.

## Someone else made a product decision for you

If your app generates copy for customers, that copy is machine-attributable by default. You should probably tell them.

## A missing watermark proves nothing

Anthropic says plainly that the metadata can be stripped by format conversion, editing or a screenshot. So if you don't find a watermark, you've learned nothing about where the content came from. Read the actual terms before someone in your company builds a detection feature on top of this.
